ABOUT ME

I am a Ph.D. student in Biostatistics at Augusta University with a background in applied statistics, data science, and mathematical sciences. My work focuses on applying rigorous statistical and computational methods to biomedical, healthcare, and real-world data problems.

I hold an M.Sc. in Applied Statistics and Data Science from the University of Texas Rio Grande Valley and a B.Sc. in Mathematical Sciences from the University of Mines and Technology, Ghana. My research interests include biostatistics, survival analysis, statistical methodology, machine learning, bioinformatics, Bayesian methods, longitudinal data analysis, stochastic processes, and statistical learning.
